Introducing AI Quests

Experiential AI literacy for every classroom

Inspired by real-world AI research

AI Quests is a game-based learning experience designed to teach middle school students (ages 11-14) about AI. Through a series of interactive, code-free quests, students will learn what it takes to responsibly build AI applications that address real-world societal challenges. The quests are inspired by actual AI projects led by Google Research.

Promotes active learning

Developed by Google Research and the Stanford Accelerator for Learning, AI Quests is designed to promote active learning. It empowers students to explore, analyze, and reflect on their own understanding as they complete each quest. This makes AI Quests a perfect complementary resource for existing in-class AI literacy programs, giving educators the flexibility to use it in whatever way supports their learning goals.

Committed to closing the literacy gap

Equipping K-12 students with AI literacy is essential for their future participation in an AI-driven world, preparing them as informed citizens, critical consumers, and potential future developers. At Google Research, we're not just developing AI tools and technologies; we're also committed to teaching students how to use AI responsibly in the world and workplace of tomorrow.
