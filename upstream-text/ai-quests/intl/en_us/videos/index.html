Students showcase: AI for societal impact
Get inspired by a new generation of problem solvers.

Watch real-world examples of students taking on the role of problem solvers, using AI to address challenges that matter to them and their communities.

Wistree

Every family has stories worth saving. Grandpa had 200 in Tamil, buried in WhatsApp, so twin 7th graders built WisTree, an app that uses AI to translate voice notes into English. (Technovation)

Food Flow

Youth winner Ahana Chatterjee from the United States coded Food Flow, helping users make smarter, healthier choices while improving food access. (MIT App Inventor)

Project Albatross

Project: ALBATROSS is a bio-inspired autopilot that teaches a small RC glider to “ride” the wind like an albatross and stretch every watt of battery power. (Raspberry Pi Foundation)

KisanSaathi

Farming can be a risky business, unless you have the right tools. Using the KisanSaathi app, farmers scan crop leaves, classify plant health, and receive personalized advice. (MIT App Inventor)

Detecting Microbursts with ML

Akshay developed a machine learning model to detect microburst damage using image analysis. Deployed on a Raspberry Pi with a camera, the system achieved 100% success. (Raspberry Pi Foundation)

Star Connect

Kids with long illnesses often cannot go to school. Star Connect is a safe space to share feelings with friends, and AI spots when someone is struggling so a parent can help. (Technovation)

Game2Life

An app created to swap screen time for offline adventures. Gemini AI personalizes activities by age and interest, mapped to Madrid's public libraries, parks and sports facilities. (Technovation)

Kinesing

Kinesign translates sign language into text and speech. A youth team from Uruguay built the app using Teachable Machine and TensorFlow to reduce communication barriers. (MIT App Inventor)

Insights Reader

Insight Reader is an automated book-flipping device that captures images of each page and uses AI to analyse the content, making physical books more interactive and accessible. (Raspberry Pi Foundation)

EcoBin

AI has some unusual uses! The image-classifier app, EcoBin, rewards households, street vendors, and restaurants that recycle used cooking oil instead of dumping it down the drain. (MIT App Inventor)

Tideguard AI

TideGuard AI is an open-source platform that forecasts where and when floating plastic will accumulate on the coast, so volunteers can clean it up before it breaks into microplastics (Raspberry Pi Foundation)

Hearo

This AI-powered phone app, called Hearo, helps children with hearing impairments improve their speech and listening skills. (MIT App Inventor)

Smart Science English

Learning science in a new language is hard when you are six. Point this app at a textbook picture and AI names it in English and says it out loud, so you can practice alone. (Technovation)

Wetland Guardian

Birds show if a wetland is healthy, and wetlands are home to 40% of all species. Fewer people are left to count them, so AI counts the birds and a game gets kids watching too. (Technovation)

EcoMonitor

EcoMonitor is an AI-driven plant health monitoring system using moisture sensors and cameras to help users maintain plant health via real-time app notifications. (Raspberry Pi Foundation)

AgeWell

Many older people in Nigeria live alone after family moves away. AgeWell is a phone app with an AI chat helper that reminds them to take medicine and calls for help if they fall. (Technovation)

RM Vision

Uzbek Sign Language users cannot always be understood. Remu uses a camera and AI to turn their signs into spoken Uzbek, and Remu Academy teaches signing through short games. (Technovation)
